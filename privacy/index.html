Iron Pack Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Iron Pack is a VR game made by WorldTurtleGod Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

What we collect

Saved game progress. Your settings, credits, unlocked parts and loadouts are saved on your headset only. We do not upload them and we cannot see them.

Meta platform info. When you play through the Meta Horizon Store, Meta may share basic info with the game, such as your user ID or username, so the game can check that you own it and show your pilot name. We use this only to run the game.

Crash and performance data. The game engine (Unity) and Meta may collect anonymous crash reports and basic device info (headset model, system version, performance) to help fix bugs. This data does not include your name or contact details.

Online play. If online matches are added, the game will send your mech's position, actions and pilot name to other players in that match through our network provider (Unity Gaming Services). This data is used only to run the match and is not sold.

We do not collect your real name, email, location, voice recordings, camera images, or payment details. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Meta (store, sign-in, ownership checks). See Meta's privacy policy at https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/
  • Unity Technologies (game engine, crash reports, online play). See https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

We only share what these services need to run the game.

How long we keep it

Your saved progress stays on your headset until you delete the game. Crash and online play data is kept by the services above under their own policies.

Children

Iron Pack is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can delete your saved progress at any time by uninstalling the game. To ask us a question or request that we delete any information tied to you, email us at the address below.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
