A cozy VR creature-catching adventure. Pick your first beast — Sparkjaw (Electric and Dragon), Shellfin (Water) or Snaplet (Ground) — then head out from the town of Cedar Bend to catch wild beasts. Sixteen elements, and every beast has its own Nature. Built in Godot 4 for PC VR via SteamVR, with a flatscreen mode too.
